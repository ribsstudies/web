Frequently Asked Questions

How do I enroll in a programme?

Contact us via phone or email, or visit our office. We'll guide you through the enrollment process and provide all necessary information.

What are the admission requirements?

Requirements vary by programme. Generally, you need a relevant degree or professional experience. Contact us for specific requirements.

Do you offer online classes?

We offer both in-person and hybrid learning options. Contact us to learn more about our flexible learning arrangements.

Are your certifications internationally recognized?

Yes! Our CILT and IPMA certifications are recognized internationally, opening doors to global career opportunities.

Can corporate organizations send employees?

Absolutely! We offer corporate training packages and can customize programmes to meet your organization's specific needs.

What is the duration of each programme?

Durations vary: CILT Certificate (6 months), Diploma (12 months), Advanced Diploma (18 months). Contact us for detailed schedules.
