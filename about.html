About RIBS

Building excellence in Logistics and Transport education since 2015

Who We Are

Integrated Business Studies & Logistics Capacity Building

RIBS is an integrated business studies and logistics Capacity Building Institution, with the main objective of providing and delivering fast, reliable and exceptionally high quality trade, transport and logistics education and services of international standard for total customer satisfaction.

It is a human capacity development, collaboration, consultancy, event/project management and recruitment outfit in the supply chain, logistics and transport sectors.

Founded: 2nd December, 2015
Incorporated: March, 2016 (Corporate Affairs Commission)

🎯

Our Vision

To remain one of the leading international trade, transport and logistics development and training institution in Nigeria and the West African Sub Region.

🚀

Our Mission

  • Develop and deliver curriculum that produces competent human resources for the industry in one stop shop
  • Keeping industry people up to date with current information and facts for desired performance
  • To help individuals and organizations realize their objective
  • Above all remain competitive in the Global World of business
Our Goals

Our Objectives

Committed to excellence in every aspect of our operations

📖

Knowledge Development

Keep industry persons, professional and practitioners/operators up to date in terms of knowledge and skill needed for industry efficiency through well packaged training programmes.

🏆

Certification Programs

Assist individuals to specialize through competency-based certification programmes and expose industry personnel to best global practices in many critical areas of industry through local and international exchange programs.

🔬

Research Access

Facilitate access to knowledge centre where industry personnel, practitioners and operators can update their knowledge and carry out research, using hardware and visual facilities.

💼

Project Management

Deploy professional competences to manage projects for cooperate organizations and relevant government bodies.

🤝

Industry Collaboration

Collaborate with professional bodies/institutions, local and international organizations to produce qualified manpower for the industry (public and private sector) through long term educational programmes.
