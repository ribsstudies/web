About RIBS

Building excellence in Logistics and Transport education since 2010

Who We Are

Integrated Business Studies & Logistics Capacity Building

RIBS is an integrated business studies and Logistics Capacity Building Institution, with the main objective of providing and delivering fast, reliable and exceptionally high quality Trade, Transport and Logistics education and services of International standard for total customer satisfaction.

We are professional trainers and consultants, especially in the areas of LOGISTICS AND TRANSPORT. We have over a decade experience as professional trainers.

We provide enterprise ideas, strategies and solutions to executive type problems and competitive challenges in real world business operations in Logistics and Transport.

🎯

Our Vision

To remain one of the leading international Trade, Transport and Logistic Development and Training Institution in Nigeria and the West African Sub-region.

🚀

Our Mission

To develop and deliver curriculum that produces competent human resources for the industry in a one stop shop. Keeping industry people up to date with current information and facts for desired performance.
